Running order with the set cues. Play full set runs the whole set from the original tracks, mixed on these cues; tap a track number to start from there. Press play on a transition to hear just that one: the outgoing track plays from 8 beats before its OUT (G), the next track comes in at its IN (E), beat-matched to the outgoing tempo and fading in over 8 beats. At the bass swap the bass moves across. The outgoing track then leaves on its FILTER (a low-pass sweep to silence over 8 beats, then a 4-beat fade) or CUT, else it fades out over 8 beats from the bass swap. On a SYNC the next track comes in at full volume, and tracks follow their JUMPs.
