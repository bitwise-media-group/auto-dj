// WHAT AUTO-DJ DOES

Rekordbox's grid and phrases plus a bass analysis in, cues where you'd put them out.

One pad layout

IN, BRIDGE, DROP, OUT and an EMERGENCY loop on the same pads on every track, replacing Mixed In Key's Energy N and Rekordbox's CUE(Auto) cues.

On the phrase

Cues snap to Rekordbox's phrase analysis, and the bass level tells breakdowns, drops and the bass swap apart. Rules are scored against your own cues with evaluate.

Harmonic key check

Every neighbour is checked on the Camelot wheel. On a clash you get the cheapest order that keeps your opener and as many pairs as it can.

Cues on both decks

G where A mixes out, E where B comes in, and BASS SWAP, FILTER and CUT markers on both tracks at the same moment, named by side.

Hear it first

Every transition and the whole set rendered from your files, following the cues exactly, at a fixed tempo with the key kept.

Safe writes

Dry runs by default. Writes need Rekordbox quit, back up master.db, apply in one transaction and verify the re-encrypted file before swapping it in.

Cue

Decode the audio once, plan the cues, check them on the review page and write them.

Cueing your library

02

Plan the set

Check the keys, take the suggested order, and render a preview of every transition.

Building a set

03

Write and play

Write the set cues and the order with Rekordbox quit, then export to your USB stick.
